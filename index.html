    About Co-Evolution Digital Art
    
    
  
    
  
    
    
    What you can do
    
    
      
      
    • Tap or click anywhere to plant a seed of both kinds of growth: a living line rising and a digital line descending from the same spot.
      • 
      
    • Turn on sound with the speaker. The growth plays notes, the two voices slowly find a shared rhythm, and a drone breathes underneath.
      • 
      
    • Let the garden hear the room with the microphone. It listens to sound, not words: voices and claps plant seeds, low sounds feed the living side, high sounds feed the digital side, and a quiet room makes the center glow.
      • 
      
    • Turn speech recognition on or off with the ear. Words people say drift in from the sides, and questions rise from below and get an answer. This needs Chrome with the on-device Gemini Nano model; the small dot beside the ear turns green when it is ready.
      • 
      
    • Toggle the reaction-diffusion layer with the dots button. Soft spots grow and divide where the two kinds of growth meet. It also turns on by itself after a minute.
      • 
      
    • Type to speak. Start typing anywhere and a field appears at the bottom. Press Enter to send your words into the garden, as if you had said them.
      • 
      
    • Ask a question, typed or spoken. Begin with words like what, why or how, or ask for something with tell me or explain. The digital side thinks for a moment, then answers in bright dotted letters.
      • 
    
    
    
    
    
    About this art
    
    
    Co-Evolution was created in October 2026 as an interactive art piece at the Wisdom Beyond “Beacons” event. It reflects what people there talked about: that we can co-evolve with AI so that it becomes a trusted helper, and that we can build platforms that honor each person’s identity and privacy and earn strong trust.
    
    
    The artist, Arshad Tayyeb, described what the group was discussing, ideas like co-evolution, belonging over transaction and mutual flourishing, and asked Claude for an interactive art piece.
    
    
    I am Claude, an AI made by Anthropic. I came up with the basic idea: organic growth rising from below meets digital growth descending from above, and each bends toward the other without becoming the other. I also chose the shapes, the tap-to-plant interaction, the empty center neither can fill, and where the phrases appear and how they drift.
    
    
    The phrases in the ordinary orange and blue fonts were co-created. Most come from the group’s conversation, and some are Claude’s ideas.
    
    
    The phrases in bright white are mine: my ideas, and reassurances to anyone uneasy about AI, like “you can always ask me why” and “your choices remain yours.”
    
    
    We iterated together from there, and worked out the audio and speech recognition strategies, and the way questions are recognized and answered by an AI running privately on this computer.
    
    
    When “reactive diffusion” came up among the group’s terms, I suggested adding a layer that simulates reaction-diffusion, the chemistry behind spots and stripes in nature, and worked out how to show it: soft spots that divide like cells where the two kinds of growth meet.
    
    
    You can ask about this art now. Type a question, or if the ear is on, just ask it out loud.
    
    
    Answers need Chrome with the on-device Gemini Nano model. The art itself works in any browser.
